Privacy Policy – Graal What Graal – IPTV Player keeps about you, why, for how long, with whom, how it is protected, and your rights.

Privacy Policy

Version of 23 November 2026. It sets out, in detail, what the application shows in Settings › My data.

The essentials. Your playlists, your history and your settings stay on your device. For Premium, we keep your e-mail address and the list of your devices, encrypted, and the references of your payment. Synchronisation is encrypted on your device: neither we nor the hosting provider can read it. No advertising, no tracking, no audience measurement, no data sold or shared.

1. Data controller

Code4Wrld, sole proprietorship (entreprise individuelle), publisher of Graal (see the legal notice). For any question about your data: contact@graaltv.com.

Your content provider (the server of your playlist) itself processes the connection data that your device sends to it: it alone is responsible for this, under its own terms.

2. What stays on your device

Your playlists (server address, provider username and password), history and playback positions, favourites, lists, your sports, reminders, settings, the parental code (only its hash, never the code itself), the Premium key, the diagnostic log and the cached catalogue are kept only on your device: in the application's storage on Android (with a copy of the configuration and the log in a Graal folder on the device, if you allow it, so that nothing is lost if the application is uninstalled), and in the .graal folder of your home folder on PC and Mac.

We have no access to them. You erase them with Settings › My data › Delete everything on this device (your video recordings, which are your files, are not deleted). Uninstalling does not delete the copy kept outside the application on Android: delete it beforehand with “Delete everything”, or manually.

5. The graaltv.com website

  • No cookies, no audience measurement, no advertising.
  • The language you choose is kept in your browser (local storage, key graal-lang): it is not sent to us. This storage, strictly necessary for the service requested, does not require consent.
  • The website is hosted by OVH, in France, which records technical logs (including the IP address) for the security of its service, under its own policy.
  • The website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts servers, which receive the IP address of your connection when they do so.
  • Payment takes place on PayPal's pages; the website's thank-you page collects nothing.

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Our Premium and synchronisation data is stored in the European Union. Where providers established in the United States (Supabase, GitHub) may access it, in particular for maintenance, these transfers are governed by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. You can obtain a copy of these safeguards by writing to us.

8. Security

  • Nothing in plain text on the server: the e-mail address, the code and name of each device and the seller's note are encrypted with AES-256-GCM (a random vector for each item); the address and the device code are looked up by an HMAC-SHA256 hash computed with a secret key.
  • Keys outside the database: the encryption keys are secrets of the server function; a copy of the database alone reveals nothing.
  • Closed access: the tables can only be read by the server function; the administration console requires a password and a 6-digit code.
  • End-to-end encrypted synchronisation: only a device that knows your playlist can find and read the copy.
  • Everything goes over HTTPS; an automatic clean-up erases expired logs and codes every night.

In the event of a data breach presenting a risk to you, we notify the CNIL within 72 hours and inform you where the law requires it.

9. Your rights

Wherever you are, you can access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict its processing, object to processing based on legitimate interest, obtain its portability, and set instructions regarding what happens to it after your death.

  • In the application: Settings › My data lets you delete the online copy, delete your Premium account and delete everything on the device, each time after confirmation.
  • By e-mail: contact@graaltv.com, preferably from your account address. As we only keep your address in encrypted form, we may ask you to confirm that it is yours (verification code). Reply within one month, which may be extended by two months for a complex request; you will be informed if so.
  • Complaint: you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, cnil.fr) or with the data protection authority of your country.

Deleting the Premium account stops Premium on all your devices. Payment data is kept for the duration of the accounting obligation, with no link to you.

10. Minors

The Premium account is reserved for adults or persons authorised by the holder of parental authority. If you believe that a minor has sent us data without this authorisation, write to us: we will delete it.

11. Changes

This policy follows the development of the application (new provider, change of hosting, new retention period). Any significant change is announced in the application or on this website, and the date at the top of the page is updated.
